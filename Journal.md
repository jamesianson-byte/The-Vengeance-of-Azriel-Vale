## Session 2 — Old Owl Well

- **Starting Location:** Secomber
- **Ending Location:** Surface camp at Old Owl Well
- **Main Objective:** Investigate Red Wizard activity and the caravan attack.
- **Key NPCs:** Tomas Reed, Marrec Thorne, Perrin Quickstep, Rowan, Hamun Kost
- **Combat Encounters:** Four animated skeletons beneath Old Owl Well.
- **Major Decisions:**
  - Rescued Rowan.
  - Investigated Hamun Kost's involvement rather than immediately treating him as an enemy.
  - Entered a temporary alliance with Hamun to examine the undead beneath the ruins.
  - Entered the underground area with Tomas, Perrin, and Hamun while Marrec and Rowan remained above.
- **Outcome:**
  - All four skeletons were destroyed.
  - Azriel suffered 7 damage.
  - The crypt was searched; no treasure, hidden passage, or further immediate threat was found.
  - The available evidence did not establish that Hamun ordered the caravan attack.
  - The party returned to the surface.
- **Resources Used:** 5 points of Lay on Hands. No spell slots or Channel Divinity used.
- **Loot and Rewards:** None.
- **XP or Milestone Progress:** No milestone reached.
- **Unresolved Threads:**
  - The wider circumstances of the caravan attack remain unresolved.
  - Hamun Kost's purpose at Old Owl Well remains under investigation.
  - The expedition must decide its next action from the surface camp.
- **Current Cliffhanger:** The party has returned to the surface at Old Owl Well.
