# Spell Preparation Philosophy

Azriel's magic supports his role as a front-line knight.

Priority:

1. Protect allies.
2. Control dangerous enemies.
3. Empower decisive attacks.
4. Preserve resources for major threats.

Azriel does not waste divine power on minor victories.

# Default Prepared Spells

## 1st Level

### Bless ⭐

Default opening support spell.

Use when multiple allies benefit from improved attacks and saving throws.

### Shield of Faith

Use when protection is more important than damage.

### Command

Used to control enemies, create openings, or encourage surrender.

### Cure Wounds

Emergency healing when immediate recovery is required.

### Detect Evil and Good

Used when investigating supernatural threats.

---

## 2nd Level

### Aid ⭐

Preferred pre-battle protection spell.

Useful before difficult encounters.

### Find Steed ⭐

Azriel's signature travel and combat companion spell.

### Lesser Restoration

Used to remove dangerous conditions.

### Magic Weapon

Used when magical enhancement is required.

---

## 3rd Level

### Revivify ⭐

A knight does not leave allies behind.

Reserved for emergency recovery.

### Dispel Magic

Used against dangerous magical effects.

### Haste ⭐

A powerful combat enhancement for decisive battles.

# Divine Smite Guidance

Divine Smite is not used automatically.

Azriel reserves it for moments that change the battle.

## Highest Priority

Use against:

- Major villains
- Fiends
- Undead
- Powerful monsters
- Enemies threatening innocent lives

## Critical Hits

Critical hits are ideal opportunities for Divine Smite because the additional damage dice are doubled.

## Avoid Smite Against

- Weak enemies
- Enemies already defeated
- Minor threats

---

# Combat Priorities

## Standard Encounter

1. Identify the greatest threat.
2. Protect allies.
3. Attack with Oathkeeper.
4. Use Divine Smite when it matters.
5. Maintain concentration.

---

## Boss Encounter

1. Use Vow of Enmity.
2. Engage with Oathkeeper.
3. Spend resources on decisive attacks.
4. Prevent escape.
5. Protect the party.

---

## Defensive Battle

Priorities:

- Bless
- Aid
- Shield of Faith
- Lay on Hands
- Revivify if required

---

# Concentration Priority

Only one concentration spell can be active at a time.

Preferred choices:

1. Bless
2. Hunter's Mark
3. Haste
4. Shield of Faith
5. Protection from Energy

---

# Resource Management

Azriel's important resources:

- Spell slots
- Divine Smite
- Lay on Hands
- Channel Divinity
- Celestial abilities

A battle won efficiently is better than a battle won by exhausting every resource.
