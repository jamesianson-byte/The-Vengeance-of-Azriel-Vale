## Common Combat Plans

### Standard Fight

- Open with Bless when multiple allies benefit and concentration is unlikely to be broken.
- Use the Attack action for two greatsword attacks.
- Preserve major spell slots against ordinary enemies.
- Use healing primarily to keep allies conscious or prevent a death spiral.

### Priority Target

- Use Vow of Enmity on the most dangerous enemy.
- Close distance and attack relentlessly.
- Spend smite resources on critical hits or decisive hits against major threats.
- Use Misty Step when terrain, distance, or enemy control prevents engagement.

### Defensive Response

- Use Aid before dangerous engagements where possible.
- Use Lesser Restoration to remove debilitating conditions.
- Use Revivify for emergency recovery.
- Use Dispel Magic against dangerous magical effects.

### Pursuit

- Use Misty Step and Relentless Avenger to prevent a marked enemy escaping.
- Use Command or Hold Person where capture is strategically better than immediate execution.

## Concentration Reminders

Only one concentration spell can be maintained at a time. Common competing choices include:

- Bless
- Shield of Faith
- Hunter’s Mark
- Hold Person
- Haste
- Spirit Shroud

## Smite Guidance

- Do not spend high-level slots on trivial enemies.
- Prioritise critical hits, fiends, undead, bosses, and enemies whose defeat immediately changes the battle.
- Retain at least one emergency resource when the full danger of an encounter is not yet known.

## Spellbook Continuity Rules

- Update this file whenever Azriel changes his default prepared spells or gains access to a new spell level.
- Record temporary preparation changes in `Save.md` if they only apply to the current adventuring day.
- Follow the exact spell wording of the chosen ruleset rather than relying on memory.
