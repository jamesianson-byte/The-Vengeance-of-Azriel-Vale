# Secomber

**Type:**  
Village / Settlement

**Region:**  
Sword Coast region

**Status:**  
Visited

**First Encounter:**  
Session 1 — In Scarlet Flames

---

## Known Facts

- Secomber is a settlement where travellers, merchants, and locals exchange information.
- The village is near the High Moor.
- Travellers pass through Secomber before entering the surrounding wilderness.
- The village has guards who monitor travellers entering and leaving.
- The village contains an inn used by locals and travellers.
- Eldrin Voss, a scholar and historian, lives in Secomber.

---

## Important Events

### Session 1

- Azriel arrived in Secomber while investigating reports of Red Wizard activity.
- Local merchants provided rumours regarding the Red Wizards.
- Village guards confirmed that two Red Wizards had entered the region.
- Azriel learned that the Red Wizards travelled toward the High Moor.
- Azriel met Eldrin Voss while seeking further information.

---

## Known NPC Connections

- Eldrin Voss — Scholar / Historian

---

## Current Status

Safe location.
