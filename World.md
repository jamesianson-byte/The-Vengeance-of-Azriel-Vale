## Current Setting

## Known Locations

### Secomber

- **Type:** Settlement
- **Known Facts:** Starting point of Azriel's investigation into reported Red Wizard activity.
- **Last Seen:** Expedition departed for Old Owl Well.

### Old Owl Well

- **Type:** Ruin / expedition site
- **Known Facts:**
  - Identified as a possible site of Red Wizard activity.
  - Hamun Kost, his apprentices, and an ogre zombie are present.
  - An underground crypt contained four animated skeletons.

- **Last Seen:** Azriel and the expedition returned to the surface camp.
- **Unresolved Threads:** Hamun Kost's purpose and the wider circumstances of the caravan attack remain under investigation.
